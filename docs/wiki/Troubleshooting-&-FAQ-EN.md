# ❓ Troubleshooting & Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

---

## 🔧 Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: Why didn't one of my games appear automatically in the library?
**A:** You can manually add any game by clicking **"+ Add Game"** on the top toolbar and selecting its executable (.exe).

### Q: Does ONYX Launcher affect in-game FPS?
**A:** No. When minimized to the tray, it uses **0% CPU** and trims RAM to **<20 MB**.

### Q: Is internet connection required?
**A:** No. ONYX Launcher works completely offline. Internet is only used for high-res logo downloads.
